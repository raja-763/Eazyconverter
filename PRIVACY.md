# Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

eazyConverter does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

## What the app does with your files

- Files you open are read into memory on your device only while they're open.
- The app never writes a copy, cache, thumbnail or history of your files.
- A file is only written when you choose to save one, to the location you pick.
- Choosing "Clear document", or quitting the app, removes the document from memory.

## Processing happens on your device

Text recognition (Apple Vision), speech (Apple's on-device voices) and language detection (Apple Natural Language) all run on your device. The app has no internet access and contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party code.

## Exported files

Exported files contain only the document's title and language as metadata. They don't include your name, device details or the time you made them.

## Clipboard

The app only writes to the clipboard when you choose Copy, and it never reads the clipboard.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this repository.
